make unnecessary.** This is the mechanism behind check 21's failure. `runGate` orders the checks

reclassification, and the only injection guarding the frozen map (injection 11, `changing a frozen

`crashing-handler-fails-open`: the case-run count against controls fell from 68 to 28 across the six
generated bundles. The commit message's claim that "retagging them near-miss/residual was the
symptom" is only half true; the retag is what makes today's bundles pass, and the detector is real
but positioned where it cannot see a future one.
